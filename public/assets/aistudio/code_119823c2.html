Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, clear answers regarding booking policies, toll calculations, and driver batta.

Booking is immediate and seamless. You can fill out the interactive booking bar on our website or directly message our central booking desk on WhatsApp at +91 7906157158. We provide vehicle availability, photo previews, all-inclusive fare options, and dispatch driver details 2 hours prior to journey.
Outstation trips are billed on an agreed per-kilometer basis with a standard minimum of 250 kilometers per calendar day. Fastag highway tolls, state border passenger tax, and parking are either transparently itemized into an all-inclusive package or billed at exact physical toll receipt charges based on your preference.
Yes! We operate dedicated one-way drop services across major North Indian corridors including Delhi to Jaipur, Agra, Chandigarh, Dehradun, Haridwar, and Lucknow at competitive fixed one-way tariffs with zero return kilometer baggage.
