## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a premium, dependable, and hospitable transport experience rooted in modern Indian travel culture. It merges professional corporate precision with inviting luxury and high-reliability logistics. The target audience spans corporate travelers, families planning pilgrimage tours or leisure excursions, and premium outstation commuters requiring trust, punctuality, and comfort.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** discipline with **Glassmorphic** and **Tactile** accents:
- **Clean Architectural Canvas:** High-legibility layouts with deliberate structural spacing, providing immediate clarity across route discovery, pricing calculators, and vehicle tier comparisons.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Navigation & Modals:** Crisp frosted overlays with backdrop blur to layer interactive trip parameters without breaking geographic or visual context.
- **Elevated Hospitality Warmth:** Strategic warm golden-amber accents juxtaposed against regal deep navy tones, projecting safety, heritage reverence, and first-class road travel.

## Colors

The color palette balances authoritative deep navy structure with vibrant royal energy and high-conversion golden-amber focal points:
- **Primary Navy (`#10243A`):** Anchors headers, primary containers, key text anchors, and vehicle specification headers, symbolizing deep trust, durability, and premium pedigree.
- **Secondary Royal Blue (`#1769AA`):** Drives dynamic interactive states, active route lines, active tabs, secondary actions, and informational tags.
- **Tertiary Golden Amber (`#F5B942`):** Reserved exclusively for conversion-critical drivers: primary booking CTA buttons, luxury tier badges, customer rating stars, and instant fare highlights.
- **Neutral Dark Charcoal (`#222B36`):** Provides sharp, WCAG AAA-compliant readability for body text and descriptive metadata without the optical harshness of pure black.
- **Backgrounds:** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary cards and floating surfaces, contrasted against a gentle cool slate wash (`#F4F7FB`) for alternate page sections, pricing tables, and dashboard wrappers.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans** to combine contemporary geometric clarity with approachable warmth. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking convey confidence, structural permanence, and modern technological efficiency.
- **Body Text:** Standard regular weight (400) paired with generous line height (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensures effortless scanning of complex booking terms, route stops, and luggage policies under bright sunlight or mobile transit conditions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in bold weights transforms category indicators, car class identifiers (e.g., "TEMPO TRAVELLER", "SEDAN LUXE"), and pricing qualifiers into crisp navigational markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to handle data-dense booking widgets, fleet comparison cards, and customer itineraries.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max content container width of 1280px centered with automatic margins. Sticky trip estimation modules occupy 4 columns while content scrolls across the remaining 8.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Fleet showcases reflow into 2-column stacked grids; fare estimation bars lock into sticky bottom sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px lateral padding. Trip booking tabs transition into horizontally scrollable pill rails with edge fades.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined dual strategy: **diffuse ambient drop shadows** tinted with navy blue undertones and **glassmorphic overlays**.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Surface container flush with canvas. Subtle 1px border colored `rgba(16, 36, 58, 0.08)`.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 36, 58, 0.06)`. Used for resting fleet cards, itinerary step modules, and review snippets.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Booking Controls):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 36, 58, 0.12)`. Elevates hovered vehicle cards, expanded dropdown menus, and active trip estimation tabs.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Booking Bars & Modals):** `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(16, 36, 58, 0.18)`. Reserved for floating booking sheets, ride confirmation dialogs, and route preview modals.
- **Glassmorphic Tier:** `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, with an inner border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Implemented on the sticky top navigation and overlay filters.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curvature that eliminates harsh digital geometry without feeling juvenile.

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers) feature a base radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Fleet display cards, trip estimation modules, and pricing comparison boxes utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to present vehicles and destinations inside refined, approachable frames.
- Chips, operational status badges (e.g., "All-India Permit", "AC Sanitized"), and micro-toggles adopt full circular pill geometry (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Book Now / Instant Fare):** Vibrant Golden Amber background (`#F5B942`), deep navy text (`#10243A`), font weight 700. On hover, subtle brightness elevation and mild expansion shadow (`0px 8px 24px rgba(245, 185, 66, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary CTA (View Fleet / Route Details):** Royal Blue background (`#1769AA`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), font weight 600.
- **Outline / Ghost Action:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in Primary Navy or Royal Blue, crisp matching text label.

### Form Inputs & Search Booking Bar
- **Input Fields:** Crisp white background with `1px solid rgba(16, 36, 58, 0.14)`. Height 48px on mobile, 52px on desktop. 
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in Royal Blue (`#1769AA`) with no optical shift. Labels shift to `label-md` floating positions.
- **Trip Parameter Switchers (One-Way / Round-Trip / Outstation):** Contained segment control with a slate surface (`#F4F7FB`), featuring an animated white pill indicator with Level 1 shadow behind the selected state.

### Vehicle Fleet Showcase Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 16px or 24px rounded white card atop `#F4F7FB` backdrop.
- **Image Module:** High-resolution vehicle cutout set against a soft radial gradient from light slate to white.
- **Specs Strip:** Icon-driven horizontal pill badges for capacity (e.g., "7+1 Seater"), fuel/transmission, luggage allowance, and air conditioning.
- **Fare Breakdown:** Bottom locked tier with per-km rates clearly visible in Charcoal (`#222B36`), accompanied by the Amber CTA.

### Route Visualizer
- **Timeline Nodes:** Custom Royal Blue start node, punctuated intermediate stop pins, and a terminal deep navy flag pin.
- **Connecting Path:** Solid 2px Royal Blue stroke for confirmed paths, dashed for optional tourist side-trips (e.g., temple stops, highway dining halts).

### Review & Rating Chips
- **Display:** Golden amber badge with SVG star icon, bold rating number (e.g., "4.9/5"), and muted count label in dark charcoal.
